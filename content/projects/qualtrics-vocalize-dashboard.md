### [](#Overview "Overview")Overview

Qualtrics has been known as a research software company. People use our products to collect data, to capture what customers say about them by doing online NPS/Customer Satisfaction surveys and website intercepts. However, data is only as valuable as the decision it enables. How to turn data into insights, into something actionable? Vocalize Dashboard brings all these data together. It is a powerful analytic tool for companies to analyze customer data, discover insights, and ultimately better understand customers.It enables users to drill down or up to check specific issues and overall trends, and solve issues at the strategic level.

  

### [](#Role "Role")Role

I led the design from end to end. Worked with UX researcher and Client Success team on usability research and customer validation.

  

### [](#Dashboard-Layout "Dashboard Layout")Dashboard Layout

A dashboard is a set of pages that have all of your customized interactive data visualizations to serve different research and analysis purposes.

![](/images/Qualtrics-Vocalize-Dashboard/Web-1280-–-3.png)

**Page Navigation:** Tabs are employed because data sets can be divided into distinct categories. Condensing information in such a way can improve the overall appearance of the display; reducing clutter on each screen and ensuring only content specific to a certain area of interest is shown within this region. Furthermore, the viewer can quickly navigate to their desired content by simply clicking on the corresponding tab. This enables them to access information without having to scan swathes of graphics, text, images and any other display media. Each tab represents a specific group of information.

**Page Filters:** Our users need the ability to interact with the dashboard and change filter settings. These are page filters that adjust all the charts on each page. When we built out filters and considered their placement, we thought page filters would be best placed at the top of the page but below page navigation, to clearly indicate the global functionality. We also designed widget level filter that works for an individual chart. Likewise, the placement of the widget filter is close to the chart with a filter badge indication so that functionality is obvious.

![](/images/Qualtrics-Vocalize-Dashboard/Web-1280-–-2-1.png)

  

### [](#Share-amp-Email-Export "Share & Email/Export")Share & Email/Export

We allow users to share the dashboard with other people in their organization. People who have access are able to adjust the filters to dive into the data that matters to them. The Share modal has the built-in data restrictions and page access settings. In this way, dashboard editors can limit the data that viewers can access. We also allow users to export data for use outside of the product. Dashboard can be exported or emailed as a PDF, JPG, PNG, or CSV file.

![](/images/Qualtrics-Vocalize-Dashboard/Share.png)

  

### [](#Widgets "Widgets")Widgets

D3.js is a JavaScript library for producing dynamic, interactive data visualizations in web browsers. It makes use of the widely implemented SVG, HTML5, and CSS standards. We used D3 to create a data visualization library. We designed more than 25 widgets, which all can be customized to present the data in a clean and desired view. Some of the widgets are interactive meaning that users can drill down into it for learning more details, interactively change what they see and how it influences the rest of the widgets on the page.

Under the dashboard editing mode, when hover on a widget, a widget frame shows up. Users are able to access all controls of the widget like resizing, moving and editing. When users try to edit a widget, the widget pops out with the editing panel sliding out. We purposely designed it this way because the indication of editing is much clear and it’s less interruptive. Users can focus on the widget itself not be interrupted by visualization flow around.
